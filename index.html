BEHIND THE NAME

A studio with heart.

Atelier Olivisa takes its name from Oliver and Isabella — two names close to the heart of the artist. “Atelier” reflects a love of French art, places and the quiet inspiration of the Mediterranean.

From oil paintings and portraits to scenes that hold memories, every piece is made with care, curiosity and a love of painting.
